    Add your YouTube cookies

    YouTube blocks downloads if bot detection triggers. Your cookies prove human browsing and stay safely local on this machine.

    1. In Chrome, Edge or Brave, install Get cookies.txt LOCALLY (opens in new tab). (Firefox: same add-on name).
    2. Open youtube.com and confirm you are signed in.
    3. Click the extension, then Export. It saves youtube.com_cookies.txt.
    4. Choose that file below or in the Connections tab.

    Cookies last a few weeks. If blocked again, re-export fresh cookies.
